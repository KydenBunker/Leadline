✦ Formatter personalization · Saved corrections ▾

Add optional text replacements for recurring shorthand. They run locally before the company-format rules. Never use them to invent appointments or change facts.

Company appointment bridge · Reference rules ▾

Before posting: Verify first and last name, 10-digit mobile, email, full Utah street address for in-person visits, complete date/year, Mountain time, and exact MEETING TYPE: In-Person or Virtual.

Designer assignment: Only use Assign to Chase (or another current designer) after confirming that designer's availability. Forced assignments bypass the calendar and pin the booking. Otherwise, use Give to Chase if available or leave it automatic.

After posting: Check the NAME: reply in the company chat (usually 5–15 minutes). If no reply after 20 minutes, contact Laurence. Editing a posted card does not update the booking.

Appointment changes: Reply in the original card thread: REASSIGN — Jane Smith — move to Carter, RESCHEDULE — Jane Smith — 3:00 PM, or CANCEL — Jane Smith. Don't repost a booked card to change it.

Photos & availability: Attach photos to the card or its thread. The company Open Times page ↗ shows available start times; designer matches can change due to travel and job complexity.

🔒 The formatter runs in your browser. Pasted lead text isn't saved or sent to Supabase or an AI provider. This is local, rule-based formatting (not generative AI). Based on the full seven-page SDR Guide v2 (Oct 14, 2026). This is a drafting and validation aid, not a booking tool. Always review and check the NAME reply after posting a card.
